# Design Patterns

Reusable, proven solutions to common software design problems. Grouped into 3 categories:

## Creational
> How objects are **created**.

- Deals with object creation in a way that's flexible and decoupled from the exact class being instantiated.
- Examples: Singleton, Factory Method, Abstract Factory, Builder, Prototype.

## Structural
> How objects/classes are **composed** into larger structures.

- Deals with relationships between objects to form bigger, flexible structures without making everything tightly coupled.
- Examples: Adapter, Decorator, Facade, Composite, Proxy, Bridge, Flyweight.

## Behavioural
> How objects **communicate and interact**.

- Deals with responsibilities and communication patterns between objects.
- Examples: Strategy, Observer, Command, State, Template Method, Iterator, Chain of Responsibility, Mediator, Memento, Visitor.
